# Liquid AI learning materials

Three study projects about Liquid AI, kept together here, separate from the benchmark. Each one is a single `index.html` file. Open it in a browser. Nothing to install.

| Folder | What it is | Built |
|---|---|---|
| `study-guide/` | **Study guide.** A long-form, textbook-style guide to Liquid AI in eight units: the technology, the market, the company, the competition, weak spots, and how it tells its story. Includes a searchable glossary and six reading paths. | June 2026, 34 commits |
| `prep-course/` | **Prep course.** The same ground as 97 short lessons in 14 modules, with a glossary, progress tracking, notes and self-test prompts. Opens behind a simple password screen. | June 2026, 29 commits |
| `edge-rush-game/` | **EDGE RUSH.** A short browser game where you play the AI chip in a phone, laptop or car. Six levels teach how on-device AI works by playing. | June 2026, 16 commits |

The study guide and the prep course cover the same material in two formats. Use the guide to read through and the course to study lesson by lesson.

Each folder has its own README with more detail.

## Where these came from

Each project began in its own repository: `NYCandrun/liquid-ai-study-guide`, `NYCandrun/liquid-ai-prep-course` and `NYCandrun/ai_game`. They were moved here with all of their commits, keeping the original messages, authors and dates. To see one project's history, run `git log --follow -- liquid-ai-learning/<folder>/index.html`.
